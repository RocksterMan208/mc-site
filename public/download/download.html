Installing the modpack

To install the modpack, you must first download it from the link provided above.


Now that the modpack/mod files are downloaded, you must ensure you have Prism Launcher installed on your system (as the modpack can only be imported through this launcher). The next step is to ensure you have logged into Prism Launcher through a microsoft account which owns a legal copy of minecraft.


The next step is to press the "New Instance" button in the top left of the Prism Launcher window. On the far left of the new window that opened up, you will see a button which says "Import". Now that you are here, you will be able to see a browse button around the middle of the screen, click this. This will then bring up a file explorer window where you can pick the modpack you downloaded previously with the link at the top of the page.

You can now click play when it has finished importing. Then please refer to the home page with instructions to connect to the server
